Isha's Curl Lab

Our mission: to help people with textured hair manage their hair in a budget-friendly and beginner-friendly way.

Made for every texture: waves, curls, coils, locs, braids and short hair. For every gender and every budget.

Prices are checked snapshots from US retailers and can change. This app gives hair-care guidance, not medical advice. Photos are analyzed in memory and never stored.
